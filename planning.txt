To address the limitations identified in existing platforms such as Fiverr, Upwork, and InDrive,
our project aims to create a web application dedicated to real-time service requests and man-
agement. This solution will allow users to post service requests for any type of task, proposing a
price and a detailed description, enabling immediate matching with available service providers.
A private communication feature will allow requesters and providers to negotiate terms,
scope, and price, ensuring clear and direct interaction. To enhance trust while respecting pri-
vacy and legality, user verification will rely on non-sensitive methods such as verified email
addresses, phone numbers, and two-factor authentication (2FA). Additionally, an optional rep-
utation system based on service history and ratings will maintain a reliable and secure envi-
ronment.
Payment handling will be flexible and secure: users can pay in cash or by credit card,
Cancellation policies are fair: requests can be canceled before agreement, and once accepted,
cancellations are only allowed if agreed upon by both parties.
